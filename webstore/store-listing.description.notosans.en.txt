This extension automatically replaces hard-to-read fonts on websites with Noto Sans JP and UDEV Gothic JPDOC.
It targets only the fonts that sites specify and that are hard to read, so text becomes easier to read without disrupting the site's layout. There is nothing to configure: just install it.

Features

・Automatic replacement just by installing
  On supported sites, fonts are replaced automatically as soon as you open a page.
  Some online services for document editing, design, coding, and chat are excluded to keep their editing experience and icons intact. Browser settings pages and other pages where extensions cannot run are also excluded.

・On/Off toggle
  Turn the extension on or off with the switch in the popup.

・Body text and monospace handled separately
  Gothic, Mincho (serif), and Latin fonts used for body text become Noto Sans JP, while monospace fonts used for code become UDEV Gothic JPDOC. Code stays aligned, and bold text (Bold) stays bold.

・Layout-friendly
  The body font is not forcibly applied to icons drawn with icon fonts or to editable areas such as input fields and editors.

・Modern web support
  Replacement also applies inside open and closed Shadow DOM and in embedded frames (including web apps such as YouTube).

・Low impact on rendering
  Replacement styles are prepared in advance and applied as the page starts loading.
  The distribution package includes only the fonts used by this edition.

・Contact form
  From the popup, you can send an inquiry to the developer (Kagayoi Support) after verifying your email address.

How replacement works

・Body text → Noto Sans JP (Regular)
  Japanese gothic and Mincho fonts specified by the site, as well as Latin and system default fonts, are replaced with Noto Sans JP. Hard-to-read Mincho text also becomes a highly legible typeface, which makes long articles more comfortable to read.

・Monospace and code → UDEV Gothic JPDOC
  Monospace fonts and code display areas specified by the site are replaced with UDEV Gothic JPDOC. Code blocks in technical blogs and similar pages also become easier to read.

  UDEV Gothic JPDOC is a monospace font that makes voiced and semi-voiced sound marks (such as "バ" and "パ") easy to tell apart, and clearly distinguishes alphanumeric characters ("1" and "l", "0" and "O").

・Characters not included in the replacement fonts (such as Arabic or Thai) are shown by the browser in the site's original font through automatic fallback.

If you want to choose fonts or the body text weight, please use the sister extension "Eye-Friendly Font Replacer", which offers a choice of 10 fonts.

How to use

1. Once installed, fonts are replaced automatically on supported sites.
2. To turn it off, open the popup from the browser toolbar (extensions menu) and flip the switch.
3. After turning it on or off, reload open pages to apply the change.

All bundled fonts are included in the extension package, and no fonts are loaded from external sources.
